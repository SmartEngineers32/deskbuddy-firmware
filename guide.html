First-Time Wi-Fi Setup for Desk Clock

You only need to do this once. The robot saves your credentials permanently in internal memory (EEPROM).

Battery & Privacy Safe: The robot only turns on its Wi-Fi for 3–5 seconds to grab live atomic time via NTP, then turns Wi-Fi completely OFF. Zero radiation, zero lag, and saves power.

1

Enter Setup Screen

In Pet Mode, double-tap the sensor (or go to Main Menu ➔ Change WiFi). The screen shows:
1. Connect to: "deskBuddy"

2

Connect from Phone / PC

Open Wi-Fi settings on your phone or laptop and connect to the hotspot named deskBuddy (no password needed).

3

Open Setup Page

A captive portal will pop up automatically! If it doesn't, simply open your browser and visit:
192.168.4.1

4

Save & Connect

Type your Home 2.4GHz Wi-Fi Name & Password, then tap "Save & Connect". The robot syncs time and returns to pet mode automatically!

Important: If Time Sync Fails on First Try

After clicking Save & Connect, double-tap the sensor in Pet Mode to open the Desk Clock. If the screen shows "Wi-Fi Not Found" or "Time Sync Failed" on your first attempt:

  1. Unplug & Re-plug USB Power: Disconnect your robot's USB power cable for 2 seconds and plug it back in. This gives the ESP8266 a clean reboot into your home network mode.
  2. Double-Tap to Fetch Time: Once powered on, double-tap the touch sensor again. The robot will now instantly connect to your Wi-Fi, fetch live atomic time in 3 seconds, and start ticking!
  3. Check Password if it Persists: If it still cannot connect, your Wi-Fi password may have had a typo. Simply repeat setup by opening Main Menu ➔ Change Wi-Fi.
💡 Tip: If you ever want to cancel Wi-Fi setup without saving, simply single-tap the touch sensor to exit safely back to Pet Mode.

Help & Troubleshooting

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question or running into an issue? Here are the most common solutions:

The clock says "Wi-Fi Not Found" or "Time Sync Failed" right after setup?
This is normal on the very first connection on some home routers. Simply unplug and re-plug the USB power cable to reboot your robot cleanly. Once powered on, double-tap the sensor — your robot will immediately connect to your saved Wi-Fi and fetch the exact time!
How do I silence the robot when studying or on a work call?
Triple-tap to open the Main Menu, scroll down to Item 6 (Idle Sound), and long-press (hold for 0.5s). A card will appear saying "Shh! Quiet mode — Silent in idle!". The robot will stay 100% silent during all background animations.
The Wi-Fi setup page didn't pop up automatically on my phone?
Some phones do not automatically launch captive portals. After connecting your phone to the Wi-Fi network named "deskBuddy", open Chrome or Safari and manually type http://192.168.4.1 into the URL address bar. The setup card will load immediately.
The touch sensor triggers randomly without me touching it?
The TTP223 touch sensor is capacitive (senses electromagnetic fields). Make sure the back of the sensor module is not resting directly on metal, laptop chassis, or messy jumper wires. If placing inside a 3D-printed enclosure, ensure the sensor pad is flush against the plastic shell.
Can I change my Wi-Fi network or password later?
Yes! Triple-tap to open the Main Menu, scroll to Item 7: Change Wi-Fi, and hold to select. The robot will launch the "deskBuddy" hotspot again so you can enter new credentials.
How do I exit a game or stopwatch back to the robot?
Inside any game or timer, a Long-Press (holding for 0.5 seconds) exits back to the menu. In Desk Clock mode, a simple Single Tap returns you straight to Pet Mode!
